About this project

ClipMate is a tiny Swift/SwiftUI menu bar app focused on speed and simplicity: pinned snippets, recent clipboard history, and quick screenshot capture without extra clutter.

Pinned Texts

Store up to four frequently used snippets and copy them in one click.

Clipboard History

Automatically track recent copied and cut text with de-duplication.

Quick Screenshots

Capture a selected area, then save to Desktop or copy directly.

Installation & running

  1. Download ClipMate.dmg from the latest release.
  2. Open the DMG and drag ClipMate into Applications.
  3. First launch: right-click ClipMate and choose Open to pass Gatekeeper for self-signed apps.
  4. The app appears in the menu bar. Open settings from the gear icon.

Want to build from source? Open ClipMate.xcodeproj in Xcode and run with ⌘R.

How to use ClipMate

  • Copy text normally (⌘C / ⌘X) to fill history.
  • Click any history item to copy it back to clipboard.
  • Pin important snippets from settings or directly from history.
  • Use screenshot action to capture an area and save or copy instantly.
